TapGrow services

QR Code Ordering Systems

Make ordering easier for customers and staff.

How it can help your business

TapGrow creates tailored digital menus and ordering websites. Customers can browse options and send orders using their own phones, whether at a table or from elsewhere when remote ordering is configured.

How it works

  1. Customers scan a table QR code or follow a website link.
  2. They browse your menu, choose items and any available extras, and send an order.
  3. Orders arrive in your business’s order-management view; optional compatible receipt printing can be configured.

Built around your business

The system is designed around your menu and workflow. Printing options depend on the tablet, printer and configuration; customers can continue paying in person if that suits your business.

Who is it for?

  • Cafés and restaurants
  • Takeaways
  • Venues wanting a simpler ordering flow
