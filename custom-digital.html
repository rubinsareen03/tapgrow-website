How it can help your business

TapGrow can create direct ordering pages for takeaways that customers open using the Website button on a business’s Google listing. They choose food and extras, place an order for collection and pay in person. We also build other custom QR and digital workflows around individual business needs.

Built around your business

For takeaways, TapGrow can build a direct online ordering page linked to the Website button on your Google Business Profile. Customers browse food, choose extras, place orders for collection and pay at the shop. We also offer custom QR-linked and digital workflows for other business needs.
