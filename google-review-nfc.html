How it can help your business

TapGrow NFC review plaques remove the awkward part. Instead of asking customers to search for your business manually, they can just tap or scan and land straight on your review page.

How it works

01

Customer taps or scans

The plaque sits on your counter, till or desk. A customer taps their phone on it or scans the QR code.

Fast and simple
02

The review page opens

Their phone opens your Google review link, so they do not need to search for your business themselves.

No app needed
03

They choose whether to review

If they want to, they can leave an honest review there and then while the experience is still fresh.

More convenience

Built around your business

We can help prepare the correct Google review link and set up a plaque ready to use. You place it where customers naturally finish their visit.

Do customers need an app?

No. Most modern phones can tap or scan and open the review page normally.

Can it include a QR code too?

Yes. That way customers can either tap with NFC or scan the QR code.
