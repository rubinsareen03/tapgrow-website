Google Review NFC Plaques

Make it effortless for customers to leave a Google review.

How it can help your business

TapGrow NFC review plaques give customers a quick way to reach your business’s Google review page. No searching for your listing or typing out a web address.

How it works

  1. Place a compact NFC and QR plaque at your till, counter or reception.
  2. Customers tap a compatible phone or scan the QR code.
  3. Their phone opens your Google review link so they can choose to leave an honest review.

Built around your business

We can help prepare the review link and configure the plaque for your Google Business Profile. Customers decide whether and what to review.

Who is it for?

  • Cafés and takeaways
  • Barbers and salons
  • Shops and other customer-facing businesses
